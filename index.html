01 — About

Why It Tastes Better

Twenty years of craft, a handshake with every farmer, and the patience to let meat taste of what it is.

🍻 Founded by Shaun Vining

Butchery the Old Way

Complete Meats has been a fixture on Honiton High Street for over twenty years. Owner Shaun Vining built the business on a simple principle: source the best meat from local farmers, hang it properly, and cut it to order — nothing less.

We work with farms across Devon and the West Country who raise their animals with care — free-range, grass-fed, and rare-breed where it makes a difference. Every piece of meat on our counter is traceable, and we’re happy to tell you exactly where it came from.

No pre-packaged shortcuts. No mystery meat. Just proper butchery, done right, served with a smile and the advice you need to cook it perfectly.
